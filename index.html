My Journey

I'm a 6th semester Software Engineering student at University of Gujrat with a CGPA of 3.54. But my real degree started at 15. I taught myself WordPress because I was tired of waiting to "learn it in class." That first site turned into 400+ projects and 2+ years of freelance work.

I'm also an entrepreneur. I co-founded "Fruit Punch" and grew it from a car-based setup to a permanent cabin in 12 months. That taught me sales, customer service, and how to build something from zero. Code taught me logic. Business taught me grit.

Creative + Technical Today.

Today I work remotely as a Live2D Rigger for international VTubers and streamers. See my rigs on Instagram @rararigz. I also create 3D visuals, edit videos, and build WordPress sites.
